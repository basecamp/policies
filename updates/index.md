# Policy update notifications have moved

This repository is no longer maintained, and watching it won't tell you about policy changes.

We post policy and subprocessor change notices at [37signals.com/privacy-updates](https://37signals.com/privacy-updates). Subscribe there to get each notice by email or feed.
